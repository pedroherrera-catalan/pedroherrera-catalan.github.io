New Economic Geography models have long treated agricultural goods as the numéraire, implicitly
assuming zero transport costs. This paper modifies Krugman's iceberg-cost formulation to introduce
agricultural transport costs and shows, for Peru, that these costs generate flows systematically
associated with flows to and from nearby regions — that is, spatial spillovers across regional
structures.
